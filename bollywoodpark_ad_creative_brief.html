Hero Claim

How It Works (Plain English)

Visitors arrive at Gate No. 1, Dadasaheb Phalke Chitranagari inside Film City Goregaon. An AC bus tour + guided walk takes them through live-use studio sets, Chroma/green-screen zones, Foley sound studios, and multiple iconic set recreations (Raj Mahal, Goa Street, Fort & Caves). Includes a live Bollywood dance & comedy show, costume try-ons, voice recording in a real studio, selfie zones with Bollywood props, and the chance to witness actual TV serial shoots if in progress.

Founder & Origin Story

The Man Who Opened Bollywood's Doors

Founder
Santosh Mijgar — Director, MOS Utility Pvt. Ltd.
Co-Enabler
Mr. Chirag Shah — Chairman, MOS Utility Pvt. Ltd. (FinTech entrepreneur; provided financial backing and institutional vision)
Origin Hook
Santosh understood the pain of thousands who came to Mumbai dreaming of Film City — but could never get inside. He decided to fix that.
Personal Pain Point
Felt a deep personal connection to the dreams of aspiring film industry hopefuls who arrived in Mumbai with nothing but a desire to see where Bollywood was made — and found the gates closed to them.
"He made it his mission to open Dadasaheb Phalke Chitranagri to the public — just to see them smile."
Credibility Marker
Promoted the park at the Cannes Film Festival. Collaborates directly with Maharashtra Government on tourism events. Named Director of MOS Utility after his tourism vision succeeded.
Launch Year
2014 — India's first and only Bollywood theme park inside Film City
Mission Beyond the Park
Increase tourism to Maharashtra; preserve and democratize India's cinematic heritage; give every Indian — regardless of background — access to the magic of Bollywood.
Business Origin
Chirag Shah launched India's first prepaid card (DONECARD) in 2005 — serial entrepreneur who extended his vision from FinTech to entertainment & cultural tourism by backing Santosh's dream.
